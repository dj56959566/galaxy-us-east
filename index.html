Protect Animals_(爱护小动物)

Respect Life · Cherish Nature · Start with Ourselves

What Can We Do?

Do not harm or capture wildlife; keep a safe distance.
Offer help to stray animals, such as food and clean water.
Adopt instead of buying—give animals a warm home.
Join animal protection activities and spread awareness.
Treat pets responsibly—never abandon or mistreat them.
